Apple Siri Integration

Control Luphonix hands-free on your iPhone 16 Pro using built-in Siri voice commands — Zero Developer Mode required!

Ready-To-Use Siri Phrases

1. Log Water by Voice Siri Voice
"Hey Siri, log 250 ml in Luphonix"

Siri triggers your Vercel cloud API and immediately updates your water ring!

2. Check Today's Tasks Siri Voice
"Hey Siri, what are my tasks in Luphonix?"

Siri reads aloud your remaining pending tasks for today.

3. Complete a Task Siri Voice
"Hey Siri, mark study completed in Luphonix"

Siri instantly marks your task as completed in cloud & on iPhone.

60-Second Setup in iPhone "Shortcuts" App

  1. Open the built-in Shortcuts app on your iPhone 16 Pro.
  2. Tap + (New Shortcut) → Name it: "Check Today's Tasks" or "Log Water in Luphonix".
  3. Add action: "Get Contents of URL".
  4. Paste your Webhook URL copied above (apiKey is already included — zero auth headers required!).
  5. Optional: Add action "Show Result" or "Speak Text" to have Siri read out your tasks aloud!
  6. Done! Say "Hey Siri, what are my tasks in Luphonix?" anytime!
